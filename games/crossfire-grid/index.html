Crossfire Grid

Place Pulse Beacons on empty tiles to fire 4-way laser beams. Hit every red Target Core on the board using your limited beacons!

How to Play

  • Goal: Hit all red Target Cores with laser beams.
  • Beacons: Tap an empty tile to place a Pulse Beacon. It fires beams Up, Down, Left, and Right.
  • Obstacles: Dark blocks stop laser beams.
  • Refund: Tap a placed Beacon to remove it and get it back.
  • Bonus: Clear levels with spare beacons for extra points!
