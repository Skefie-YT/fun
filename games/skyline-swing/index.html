How to Play

  • Grapple: Press & hold Mouse, Touch, or Space to tether to the nearest overhead anchor.
  • Momentum: Swing downward to gain speed, then release near the upswing to launch far!
  • Speed Rings: Fly through glowing cyan rings for +50 pts and a burst of speed.
  • Survive: Don't fall below the bottom of the screen!
