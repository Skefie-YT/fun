Sunburst Bastion

Aim your solar cannon to blast incoming void waves, collect Solar Shards, and build orbital turrets on the 6 sockets around your core.

Aim: Mouse / Touch • Build Turret: Tap Socket (25✦) • Overdrive: Space

How to Play

  • Aim & Fire: Move your mouse or drag your finger to aim the central Solar Cannon. It fires automatically!
  • Orbital Turrets: Tap any of the 6 glowing sockets around the core to build a turret (25✦) or upgrade it up to Lv.3.
  • Overdrive: Hitting enemies charges your Solar Overdrive. Press Space or tap the Overdrive button to unleash a 360° shockwave!
  • Survive: Don't let void invaders or boss orbs breach your core shield.
