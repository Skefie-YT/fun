How to Play

Vector Sling

  • Aim & Launch: Drag anywhere on the screen to pull back your slingshot. Release to fire the kinetic orb.
  • Shatter Crystals: Hit all glowing cyan crystals in the sector before your shots run out.
  • Gravity & Bumpers: Purple wells curve your trajectory; amber bumpers give extra bounce and points!
  • Combos: Shatter multiple crystals in a single shot for a massive combo multiplier. Unused shots grant bonus points!
