How to Play

  • Goal: Strike every glowing beat node on the ring as the sweeping crimson needle passes over it.
  • Controls: Tap anywhere on the screen, click, or press Space / Enter.
  • Combo & Fever: Consecutive hits increase your multiplier. Gold nodes award bonus points!
  • Shields: You have 3 shields. Missing a node or tapping empty space costs 1 shield.
