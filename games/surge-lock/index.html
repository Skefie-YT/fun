HOW TO PLAY

  • Lock Nodes: Tap, click, or press Space when the orb is inside a glowing teal arc.
  • Perfect Center: Hit the bright white core of an arc for bonus points!
  • Reverse & Speed: Each lock flips your orbit direction and increases speed.
  • Shields: You have 3 shields. Missing a lock costs 1 shield and resets your combo.
  • Fever Mode: Reach a 10x combo for double points!
