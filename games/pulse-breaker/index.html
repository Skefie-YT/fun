How to Play

  • Smash the Beat: Tap the 4 lane pads or press D, F, J, K (or 1, 2, 3, 4) when blocks enter the Strike Zone.
  • Build Combos: Consecutive hits increase your multiplier up to 8x and restore shields every 25 hits.
  • Gold Blocks: Worth 2x points and charge your Hyper Pulse meter faster.
  • Hyper Pulse: When at 100%, press Space or tap the top bar to auto-smash all blocks for 4 seconds!
