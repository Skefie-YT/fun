How to Play

1. Click or tap any empty tile to place a Cryo-Barrier.
2. Immediately after, the Fire spreads 1 tile orthogonally (up, down, left, right).
3. Fuel tiles explode outward in all 4 directions when ignited!
4. Completely trap the fire so it has nowhere to spread to clear the wave.
