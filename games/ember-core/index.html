Ember Core

Shadow shards are closing in! Drag the glowing Ember Core to power nearby sentry towers. Only powered towers can fire.

Drag or Move Mouse to reposition the Core

How to Play

1. The Core (Center) powers towers in its radius.
2. Towers automatically target the nearest enemy.
3. Don't let enemies touch the Core!
4. Stay close to towers to keep them firing.
