SHIELD BOT

Protect the central crystal. Drag bots to block incoming projectiles. Don't let your core take 3 hits!

PC: Drag with Mouse | Mobile: Drag with Finger

How to Play

• Waves of red drones will fire at your core.
• Drag your green Shield Bots into the line of fire.
• Each bot absorbs 5 hits before needing a 3s recharge.
• If the core gets hit 3 times, game over!
• Survive waves to earn bots and score points.

SYSTEM FAILURE

You protected the core for 0 waves.
