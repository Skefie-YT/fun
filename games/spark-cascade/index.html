Spark Cascade

Click any tile to detonate it and fire 4-way Spark Waves. When a wave hits a matching color, it chain-reacts across the board!

How to Play

  • 1. Detonate: Tap any tile to fire 4 directional Spark Waves (Up, Down, Left, Right).
  • 2. Chain Reaction: Waves pass through different colors, but detonate any tile of the same color!
  • 3. Bonus Moves: Trigger a chain of 6+ tiles for +1 Move, or 12+ tiles for +2 Moves.
  • 4. Prism Bomb: Chains of 8+ spawn a glowing Prism tile that blasts all neighbors!

Out of Moves!

Your spark energy has faded, but the cascade lives on.
