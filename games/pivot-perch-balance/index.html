PIVOT PERCH

Drop dynamic weights onto a sensitive seesaw beam. Counterbalance torque, stack blocks high, and avoid beam tipping or letting blocks tumble off!

• Tap / Click / Space: Release the hanging weight.
• Move Cursor / Touch: Steer the drop crane.
• Torque Law: Further from fulcrum = stronger tilt force!
• Tilt Meter: Keep tilt inside ±32° or beam collapses.

HOW TO PLAY

• Goal: Stack as many weights as possible onto the rocking beam.
• Torque Physics: Mass × Distance creates rotational inertia.
• Multipliers: Near-level balance (+/- 4°) awards 2x Combo points!
• Game Over: If the beam exceeds ±32° or 3 blocks tumble into the abyss.
• Controls: Aim with cursor or drag horizontally, tap/click/Space to release.
