How to Play

  • Steer: Use A / D or Arrow Keys (or on-screen buttons) to move the crane trolley left and right.
  • Drop: Press Space or tap DROP to release the magnetic grapple.
  • Physics: Moving the trolley makes the cable swing! Counter-steer to steady the crate before dropping.
  • Perfect Stack: Land a crate within tight alignment for a SNAP bonus and combo multiplier!
