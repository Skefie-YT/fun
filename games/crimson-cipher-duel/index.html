How to Play

1. Watch (Encode): The AI flashes a sequence of nodes on the 4×4 grid.
2. Decode (Duel): Tap the nodes in the exact same order before the timer bar runs out.
3. Reverse Rounds: Every few rounds, a REVERSE alert appears—input the sequence backwards!
4. Shields: You have 3 shields. Wrong taps cost 1 shield. Speed earns bonus points!
