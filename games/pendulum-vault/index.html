How to Play

Controls & Rules

1. Jump: Tap, Click, or press Space / Up when on a platform to leap.
2. Grapple: Press and Hold in mid-air near a glowing cyan node to latch on and swing.
3. Launch: Release at the bottom or upswing of your arc to launch forward at high speed!
4. Shards: Collect crimson energy shards for bonus points. Don't fall into the void!
