Tidal Stack

Drop glowing blocks onto a wave-tossed barge. Balance the center of mass and stack as high as you can without capsizing!

How to Play

  • Aim & Drop: Move with Mouse, Touch, or A/D / Arrow keys. Click, Tap, or press Space to drop.
  • Balance: Every block adds weight to its side of the barge. Keep the stack centered so the waves don't tip it!
  • Perfect Drops: Align closely with the block below for bonus points and combo multipliers.
  • Lives: You have 3 lives. Losing a block to the sea costs 1 life.
