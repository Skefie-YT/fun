SHARD SENTRY

Incoming void shards are targeting the core. Rotate your sentry guards to block them! Do not let them breach the center.

HOW TO PLAY

1. Shards fly in from all directions.
2. You control twin sentries orbiting the core.
3. Rotate to hit shards with sentries.
4. Missing one shard ends the sequence.
