Prism Ripple

Detonate any tile to fire 4-way color ripples across the grid. Matching tiles hit by a ripple explode too, sparking massive chain reactions!

How to Play

  • Detonate: Tap or click any tile to pop it and send 4-way ripple beams (up, down, left, right).
  • Chain Reaction: Any tile of the same color hit by a ripple also detonates and fires its own ripples!
  • Prism Wildcard: Rainbow tiles match all colors and supercharge your chain.
  • Clear the Board: Pop all tiles before running out of moves to advance and earn bonus moves!

Out of Moves!

Great chain reactions! Ready for another run?
