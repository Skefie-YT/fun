Aegis Bastion

Defend the central Aegis Core from relentless waves of void invaders. Aim your manual pulse cannon and deploy upgradable turrets on the 6 orbital sockets!

How to Play

  • Aim & Fire: Move your mouse or drag on touchscreens to aim and auto-fire your Core's Pulse Cannon.
  • Build Turrets: Click or tap any glowing empty socket around the core to build a Pulse Turret (50 Energy).
  • Upgrade: Click an existing turret to upgrade it up to Level 3 for faster fire & multi-shots!
  • Nova Blast: Destroying enemies charges your Nova meter. Press Space or tap NOVA BLAST to unleash a screen-clearing shockwave.
