Cascade Core

Swap adjacent energy tiles to match 3 or more, trigger massive gravity cascades, and detonate Nova Cores before your moves run out!

How to Play

  • Swap: Tap a tile then an adjacent tile, or swipe between them.
  • Cascade: Falling tiles that form new matches multiply your score!
  • Special Cores: Match 4 in a line for a Cross Blaster; match 5+ for a Nova Core (3×3 blast).
  • Bonus Moves: Trigger a x3+ Cascade to earn +1 extra move!
