How to Play

Chrono Snap

  • Goal: Snap the needle inside the glowing cyan arc to score and reverse direction.
  • Perfect Hit: Land near the center of the arc for bonus points and combo multipliers.
  • Fever Mode: Reach a 10x combo to ignite Fever Mode for double points!
  • Shields: You have 3 shields. Missing the arc costs 1 shield and resets your combo.
  • Controls: Tap anywhere, click, or press Spacebar / Enter.
