SPECTRA SPARK

Tap tiles to charge them. When they reach level 4, they explode, sending sparks that charge neighboring tiles!

HOW TO PLAY

  • Tap a tile to increase its level.
  • Level 3 tiles explode when tapped.
  • Explosions send 4 sparks (Up, Down, Left, Right).
  • Chain reactions do not cost extra taps!
  • Clear 10 tiles to gain +2 bonus taps.
