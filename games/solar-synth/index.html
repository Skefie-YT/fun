How to Play

Solar Synth

  • Goal: Hit incoming beat orbs right as they cross the glowing resonator rings.
  • Controls: Press A / S / D, ← / ↓ / →, or tap/click the 3 pads directly.
  • Solar Flare: Accurate hits charge your Flare meter. At 100%, Solar Flare activates automatically for 2x Score!
  • Stability: Missing orbs drains Core Stability. Perfect hits restore a little stability.
