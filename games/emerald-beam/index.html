Emerald Beam

Rotate tactical mirrors on the grid to guide the emerald laser into every crystal target before your moves run out.

How to Play

  • Goal: Light up every emerald crystal on the grid with the laser beam.
  • Rotate: Tap or click any mirror tile (/ or \) to flip its angle 90°.
  • Efficiency: Clear sectors with spare moves for bonus points!
  • Controls: Mouse / Touch on tiles, or Arrow Keys + Spacebar.

Out of Moves

The laser grid locked down before all crystals were energized.
