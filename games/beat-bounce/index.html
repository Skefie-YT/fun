Beat Bounce

Your orb arcs from pad to pad on the beat. Tap right as the shrinking ring hits the pad to chain combos and unleash Fever Mode!

How to Play

Rules & Controls

  • Timing: Tap, click, or press Space when the orb lands on a pad and the golden ring aligns.
  • Combos: Hit Perfect or Good beats to build your multiplier. Reach 8x combo for Fever Mode!
  • Shields: You have 3 shields. Missing a beat or tapping way too early costs 1 shield.
  • Tempo: The BPM gradually speeds up as your score climbs!
