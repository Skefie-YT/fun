Fulcrum Fall

Tap to drop blocks and stack them high.
Keep the beam balanced or the tower will tumble!

TAP or CLICK to drop blocks

How to Play

1. Blocks move side-to-side at the top.
2. Tap to drop a block onto the rocking beam.
3. Every block has weight. Keep the beam level.
4. Game ends if any block falls off the platform.
