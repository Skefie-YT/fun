POLAR FLIP

Ride the neon rail and flip between top and bottom polarity. Dodge plasma hazards, grab energy orbs, and time close calls for massive combos!

How to Play

  • Controls: Tap anywhere, Click, or press Space / Up / Down to flip sides.
  • Dodge: Avoid red plasma spikes and laser gates on your side of the rail.
  • Collect: Grab gold energy orbs to boost your score.
  • Close Call: Flipping right before a hazard triggers a +25 bonus and increases your combo multiplier!
