Cinder Sling

Pull back to launch your ember orb. Bank off walls to boost your multiplier and shatter every ice crystal before your shots run out!

How to Play

🔥 Aim & Sling: Drag anywhere and release to launch the ember orb.
💎 Ice Crystals: Shatter all cyan crystals to clear the level.
⚡ Bank Multiplier: Every wall bounce before a hit increases your score multiplier (x2, x3, x4...).
🟠 Magma Bumpers: Supercharge your orb's speed and add +1x multiplier!
🧊 Fragile Glass: Shatters on impact to open new ricochet paths.

Out of Shots!

The ember cooled down before all crystals were shattered.
