How to Play

Laser Lattice

Goal: Survive as long as possible while collecting glowing Emerald Shards.
Telegraphs: Thin dashed lines warn where a high-energy laser will fire next.
Phase Dash: Press Space, Click, or tap DASH for a brief burst of speed and invulnerability to cross active beams!
Graze Bonus: Skimming close to active lasers or dashing through them boosts your score multiplier.
