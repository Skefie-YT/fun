How to Play

  • Each click costs 1 move.
  • Tiles go from Gray → Blue → Red → Yellow (Max).
  • Click a Yellow tile to explode it 4-ways!
  • Explosions level up other tiles for free.
  • Earn +1 move for every 5 tiles cleared in one chain.
