HOW TO PLAY

  • Goal: Create a continuous path from the left edge to the right edge.
  • Controls: Click/Tap a tile to shift its Row. Holding Shift or long-pressing shifts the Column.
  • Bonus: Longer paths yield more points.
  • Speed: The timer gets faster as you level up!
