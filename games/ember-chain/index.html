Ember Chain

Ignite any tile to spark a chain reaction through matching neighbors. Bigger chains forge Ember Cores and refund moves!

🔥 Ignite: Tap a tile to detonate it and all connected matching tiles.
⚡ Ember Core: Chains of 5+ forge a Core that blasts a 3×3 area!
🎯 Move Refund: Chains of 4+ grant +1 Move; 7+ grant +2 Moves.
How to Play

Ember Chain

🔥 Goal: Score as many points as possible before running out of moves.
💥 Chain Reactions: Clicking a tile ignites it, spreading to orthogonally adjacent tiles of the same color.
✨ Ember Cores: Clearing 5+ tiles in one move spawns a glowing Ember Core. Click it to blast a 3×3 zone and trigger nearby matches!
🎮 Controls: Click/Tap any tile, or use Arrow Keys + Space/Enter.
