How to Play Pulse Forge

  • Goal: Strike molten energy orbs right as they enter the glowing Left or Right anvil rings.
  • Controls: Tap the Left or Right side of the screen, or press A / ← and D / →.
  • Dual Beats: When orbs approach both sides together, press Space or tap both sides!
  • Forge Fever: Hit consecutive beats to raise your multiplier up to 4x. Missing drains Forge Integrity (5 max).
