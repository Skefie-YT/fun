Quantum Knight

Leap across the neon grid in L-shaped knight moves to capture every Data Core before your energy runs out.

How to Play

  • Move: Tap any highlighted green tile to leap in an L-shape (2 squares + 1 perpendicular).
  • Goal: Capture all glowing Data Cores before your Energy reaches 0.
  • Cracked Tiles: Dashed amber tiles collapse into void after you leap off them.
  • Bonus: Unused Energy carries into your score!
